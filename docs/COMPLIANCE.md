# Compliance Workstream

Status: **REQUIRES BULGARIAN LEGAL + ACCOUNTING SIGN-OFF**

## Confirmed high-level EU baselines

- Bulgaria adopted EUR on 1 Jan 2026.
- EU distance sales generally provide a 14-day withdrawal right for consumers, with defined exceptions; disclosures affect who pays return postage.
- VAT OSS can simplify qualifying EU B2C distance-sale VAT reporting.
- GPSR/product-safety obligations can apply depending on Koli Parts' role and product categories.

These are not sufficient to determine Koli Parts' exact obligations.

## Decisions to obtain in writing

### Merchant model
Is Koli Parts seller/merchant of record, agent/intermediary, affiliate/referral service, or a hybrid by supplier channel?

### VAT/invoicing
- Bulgarian VAT registration status.
- OSS use and thresholds/rules applicable to chosen model.
- invoice issuer.
- supplier invoice booking.
- German hub effects if used.

### Consumer rights
- withdrawal policy and disclosures,
- return cost allocation,
- defective/wrong-fit remedies,
- used-parts conformity/warranty treatment,
- diminished-value handling.

### Product safety
Classify airbags/pyrotechnics, batteries, oils/fluids, braking/safety-critical parts, electrical goods and used parts before listing them.

### Data/brand
- eBay data retention/licensing,
- supplier images/descriptions,
- TecDoc licensing,
- VIN personal-data assessment,
- privacy/cookie policies.

## Rule

Never encode “electronic parts cannot be returned” or similar blanket exclusions without a verified legal basis. Use policy rules approved for the exact category and reason.
